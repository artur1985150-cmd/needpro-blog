In short

  • American, Delta and United cap a carry-on at 22 x 14 x 9 inches (about 56 x 35 x 23 cm), wheels and handles included.
  • Southwest is larger: 24 x 16 x 10 inches. Delta also sets a 45-linear-inch total.
  • United Basic Economy allows only a personal item on most routes, so a carry-on of any size has to be checked.
  • Budget airlines measure smaller and weigh: Ryanair's free bag is 40 x 30 x 20 cm, and AirAsia caps both cabin bags at 7 kg together.
  • What you pack still has rules: liquids in containers of 3.4 oz or less in one quart bag at TSA.

On American, Delta and United, a carry-on bag can be up to 22 x 14 x 9 inches (about 56 x 35 x 23 cm), and the wheels and handles count. Southwest allows more, 24 x 16 x 10 inches. Budget airlines are stricter: Ryanair's free bag is only 40 x 30 x 20 cm, and some carriers weigh what you bring on board. Each airline sets its own limit, so check the one on your ticket.

The 22 x 14 x 9 standard, airline by airline

There is no federal carry-on size. TSA screens what is inside your bag; the airline decides how big the bag may be. The numbers below come from each airline's own baggage page, read on November 11, 2026.

Notice that the same 22 x 14 x 9 inches turns into 36 cm of width on American's page and 35 cm on Delta's and United's. If you are buying a bag to work on all three, the smaller metric figure is the safe one.

Wheels and handles count

Every airline in the table measures the outside of the bag. American's carry-on "cannot exceed 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm) including handles and wheels." United asks you to "be sure to include the handle and wheels." Southwest says "wheels, handles, and any attachments count toward these dimensions."

So a "22-inch" label on a suitcase only helps if that figure includes the wheels and handle. If the label does not say, measure the bag yourself, standing up, from the floor to the top of the folded handle.

Delta adds a second test: length, width and height together "must not exceed 45 linear inches (114 cm)." A bag of exactly 22 x 14 x 9 adds up to 45, so it passes both.

Basic economy and other fare traps

Size is only half the question. Your fare decides whether you may bring a carry-on at all.

United's page is direct about Basic Economy: "For most trips you can only bring one personal item when you're flying in Basic Economy. You can't bring a carry-on bag unless you're flying to South America, across the Atlantic or across the Pacific." United makes exceptions for MileagePlus Premier members, Star Alliance Gold members and holders of qualifying United credit cards.

On Ryanair, the 40 x 30 x 20 cm bag under the seat is all a basic fare includes. The 10 kg cabin bag of 55 x 40 x 20 cm comes with the paid "Priority & 2 Cabin Bags" option, which also lets you board first.

So before you buy a bag, look at the fare. A perfect 22 x 14 x 9 suitcase still gets checked if your ticket only covers a personal item. The personal item has its own limits, which we cover in our guide to personal item size.

Weight limits: the budget airline catch

The American, Delta, United and Southwest carry-on pages we read set sizes, not weights. Low-cost carriers often care more about the scale.

AirAsia's rule, from its newsroom advisory: each guest may bring one cabin bag and one small personal item "with a combined total weight of 7kg." AirAsia adds that "all items purchased at the airport form part of the cabin baggage allowance," apart from sealed duty-free bags with proof of purchase. Bags over the size or weight limit must be checked in at the counter; if they reach the gate, "a gate baggage fee may apply."

Ryanair sets its larger cabin bag at 10 kg. If your trip mixes a US flight with a budget hop in Europe or Asia, pack to the smallest allowance on the itinerary. For bags that go in the hold, see our article on checked bag weight limits.

What happens if your bag is too big

Airlines check bags in sizers at the airport. American says your bag "must fit in the airport sizer," and United keeps bag sizers at the airport for anyone unsure. If it does not fit, the bag goes in the hold, and you may pay a checked bag fee. United's page, read November 11, 2026, says that for tickets bought on or after April 3, 2026, bag fees start at $45 if you prepay, $50 in the lobby and $75 at the gate.

There is one free version of gate checking. On American Eagle regional flights with small overhead bins, American says a carry-on larger than the personal item dimensions must be valet-checked: "We'll tag and check your bag at the gate for you at no charge" and hand it back on the jet bridge. United also warns that bags are gate-checked most often on United Express flights.

Before any gate check, take out what must stay in the cabin. American and United both say batteries and e-cigarettes cannot go in checked bags, and United tells you to keep medication and keys in your personal item for this reason.

Packing the bag itself

A carry-on that fits the sizer still passes through TSA. Liquids, aerosols, gels, creams and pastes have to be in containers of 3.4 ounces (100 ml) or less, all in one quart-sized bag, and TSA says larger containers belong in checked baggage. Our article on the TSA liquid rule covers the exceptions for medicine and baby formula. Bags that need a second look slow you down; see how early to arrive at the airport for the airlines' cutoff times.

A quick check before you fly

  1. Measure the bag with wheels and handle: 22 x 14 x 9 inches or less for most big US airlines, 24 x 16 x 10 for Southwest.
  2. Read your fare: Basic Economy on United means a personal item only on most routes.
  3. On budget airlines, weigh both bags together.
  4. Put liquids in containers of 3.4 ounces or less in one quart bag.
  5. Keep batteries, medicine and keys in the bag that stays with you.

These are the airlines' rules as we read them on November 11, 2026. Airlines change baggage rules and fees, and Southwest notes that a partner airline on your itinerary may have different carry-on rules, so check the page of the airline flying each leg before you pack.

Questions people ask

What is the standard carry-on size?

On American, Delta and United it is 22 x 14 x 9 inches, including wheels and handles. That is the closest thing to a US standard, but it is set by each airline, not by law.

Is a 22-inch suitcase a carry-on?

Only if the whole bag, wheels and handle included, stays within 22 x 14 x 9 inches on those airlines. If the label does not say whether wheels are included, measure the bag yourself.

Is Southwest's carry-on size bigger?

Yes. Southwest's help page gives 24 x 16 x 10 inches, with wheels, handles and attachments counted in the dimensions.

Can I bring a carry-on in basic economy?

It depends on the airline. United says Basic Economy passengers can only bring one personal item on most trips, with a carry-on allowed only on flights to South America, across the Atlantic or across the Pacific. Check your fare rules before you pack.

Do airlines weigh carry-on bags?

The big US airlines' carry-on pages we read give sizes, not weights. Budget carriers often do weigh: AirAsia allows 7 kg for the cabin bag and personal item together, and Ryanair's larger cabin bag is a 10 kg bag.
