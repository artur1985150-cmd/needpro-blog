To change your App Store country on iPhone, go to Settings, tap your name, then Media & Purchases, then Country/Region. It only works once Apple's conditions are met: your account balance is spent, subscriptions that block the change are canceled and have run out, and pre-orders, rentals and refunds are finished. You may also need a payment method from the new country. Apple's page was checked on 30 October 2026.

When you need to change the country, and when you don't

Your Apple Account has one country, and when you change it Apple asks for a payment method and billing address from the new one. Apple's support page treats this as part of moving: you now live somewhere else and want that country's store.

A trip is different. If you live in the EU and spend a few weeks in another EU country, the EU portability rule already requires paid online content services, from video to music and e-books, to work as at home with no extra charge. We explain that rule in our pillar on using Netflix and other subscriptions in another country. For a holiday, there's nothing to change.

Before you start: Apple's checklist, in order

Do these while your old card still works. A canceled subscription has to run to the end of its billing period, so start at least one period before the move, not on the last day.

  1. Spend your Apple Account balance on the App Store or other Apple services. Apple says this first: any balance left can stop the change.
  2. Cancel subscriptions that block a country or region change, then wait until the end of the current subscription period. Canceling isn't enough on its own; the period has to run out.
  3. Wait for memberships, pre-orders, movie rentals and Season Passes to complete, and for pending store credit refunds to process. You can cancel a pre-order if you don't want to wait.
  4. Check Family Sharing. Apple says that if you're part of a Family Sharing group, you might not be able to change your country or region.
  5. Have a valid payment method for the new country ready. Apple says you "might need" one.
  6. Redownload your apps, music, movies, TV shows and books on every device you plan to use. Some content might not be available in the new country.

One side effect of step 2: if you cancel Apple One or iCloud+, your iCloud storage drops to the free 5 GB plan. Back up or tidy what you keep in iCloud before you cancel. Once the new payment details are in, you can subscribe again in the new country, if the service is offered there.

How to change App Store country on iPhone or iPad

Apple's steps for iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision Pro, as published on 30 October 2026:

  1. In Settings, tap your name.
  2. Tap Media & Purchases. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account.
  3. Tap Country/Region.
  4. Select your new country or region and tap Continue.
  5. Review the Terms & Conditions, then tap Agree.
  6. Select a payment method, enter your new payment information and billing address, then tap Done.

On a Mac, a Windows PC or the web

Device Where to go Then
Mac Apple Music or Apple TV app, Account menu, Account Settings Country/Region, choose the country, Continue, agree, enter payment and billing, Done
Windows PC Apple Music or Apple TV app, your name in the sidebar, View My Account Change Country or Region, choose the country, agree, enter payment and billing, Continue

What if there is still money on the account?

Apple puts the balance first on its list: money left on the account, even a small amount, can stop the change.

Spend it if you can. If the balance is less than the cost of a single item in the App Store or other Apple services, Apple says to contact Apple Support to clear it.

Don't count on getting it back as cash. Apple's page says gift cards, App Store gift cards and unused Apple Account balance can't be redeemed or returned for cash, "except as required by law". If your country's law allows a refund of redeemed gift balances, you can ask Apple Support, and Apple asks you to include the primary email address of your Apple Account.

Why can't I change my App Store country?

If the Country/Region option refuses to go through, check Apple's conditions one by one:

What's blocking it What to do

A subscription that blocks the change Cancel it and wait for the period to end
A pre-order, rental, Season Pass or refund still pending Wait for it to finish, or cancel the pre-order
You're in a Family Sharing group Apple says you might not be able to change the country; you may need to leave the group
No payment method from the new country See the next section

No card from the new country yet?

A local bank card can take time to arrive after a move. Apple's page covers this case: if you don't want to enter a payment method, wait until you're physically in your new country or region, then create a new Apple Account.

That means a separate account, not your old one with a new country. Before going that way, redownload what you need on the old account, as in step 6 above.

Will you lose your apps and purchases?

How-to blogs disagree here, so stick to what Apple says. Apple warns that some types of content might not be available in your new country, and it tells you to redownload your apps, music, movies, TV shows and books on any devices you might use to play or read them in the future. Do that before you switch, not after.

A moving checklist for your phone

The App Store country is one of several things tied to your old country. While you sort it out:

  1. Finish Apple's checklist above while your old card still works.
  2. Look at other subscriptions with a country attached; Netflix, for example, asks you to cancel and sign up again in the new country.
  3. Check that your phone can take a local eSIM: see does my phone support eSIM.
  4. Moving to Turkey? A phone brought from abroad needs registration to keep working on Turkish SIM cards; details in Turkey IMEI registration.

Questions people ask

How do I change my App Store country on iPhone?

Open Settings, tap your name, then Media & Purchases (sign in if asked), then Country/Region. Pick the new country, tap Continue, agree to the Terms & Conditions, and enter a payment method and billing address for the new country.

Why can't I change my App Store country?

Usually one of Apple's conditions isn't met: there is still money on the account, a subscription hasn't reached the end of its period, a pre-order, rental or refund is pending, or you are in a Family Sharing group. Apple's page lists these as the things to check first.

What if I have a few cents left on my Apple Account?

If the balance is less than the cost of a single item, Apple says to contact Apple Support to clear it. Apple doesn't pay out gift cards or account balance in cash, except where the law requires it.

Will I lose my apps if I change country?

Apple warns that some apps, music, movies, TV shows and books might not be available in the new country, and tells you to redownload them on every device you'll use before you switch.

Do I need to change the country for a short trip?

No. Apple's page is about moving. And if you live in the EU and travel to another EU country for a while, EU rules require paid content subscriptions to work as they do at home.
