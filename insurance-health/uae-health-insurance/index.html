In short

  • Dubai's single-entry tourist visa lists medical insurance valid in the UAE as a condition (GDRFA Dubai, checked 30 October 2026).
  • The five-year multiple-entry tourist visa needs a policy applicable in the UAE valid for 180 days, plus a USD 4,000 bank balance over six months.
  • In Abu Dhabi, employers and sponsors insure employees and their families: one spouse and three children under 18.
  • In Dubai, employers insure employees; sponsors insure their resident dependants.
  • Since 1 January 2025 employers must buy a policy before a private-sector employee's or domestic worker's residency is issued or renewed.

Yes, in most cases. If you live in Abu Dhabi or Dubai, your employer or sponsor must insure you. If you need a tourist visa, Dubai's single-entry visa and the five-year multiple-entry visa both list a medical policy valid in the UAE. That is what the government portal u.ae and GDRFA Dubai said on 30 October 2026.

Most pages that rank for this question are brokers selling plans. We read the official pages instead. Visitors and residents are handled separately, since the rules differ and so does the person who pays.

Do tourists need health insurance for a UAE visa?

Two official pages answer this, for two different visas.

Dubai's General Directorate of Identity and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA) lists three conditions for its single-entry tourist visa: a passport valid for at least six months, a ticket to continue or leave the UAE, and "Medical insurance valid in UAE". The visa is for 30 or 60 days, and only accredited tourism offices in the emirate can issue it.

The u.ae portal, updated on 1 October 2026, lists the conditions for the multiple-entry five-year tourist visa, open to all nationalities:

  • a bank balance of USD 4,000 or its equivalent over the last six months;
  • a valid health insurance policy applicable in the UAE, valid for 180 days;
  • an onward or return ticket out of the UAE.

One detail worth knowing: the u.ae tourist visa page itself mentions insurance only under the five-year visa. The single-entry requirement appears on the GDRFA Dubai service page. If your visa is filed through another emirate's authority, read that authority's own service page.

What does a tourist visa cost, and who files it?

The GDRFA Dubai service page gave these fees on 30 October 2026:

Single-entry tourist visa (GDRFA Dubai) Fee
30 days AED 200 + 5% VAT
60 days AED 300 + 5% VAT
Extra if the visitor is already inside the UAE AED 500, plus AED 10 Knowledge Dirham and AED 10 Innovation Dirham

The page adds that the total may vary depending on the visitor's circumstances.

You do not apply at an embassy. The u.ae portal says UAE embassies do not issue tourist visas: a UAE-based airline, a licensed travel agent or your hotel applies for you. That is also who to ask whether the policy you already have is accepted, or whether they arrange one.

Is a worldwide travel policy enough?

The test on both pages is the same: the policy must be valid, or applicable, in the UAE. A policy that excludes the Gulf or the Middle East fails it whatever its limit. Before you rely on a policy from home, check its territory list for the UAE by name or by region.

The five-year visa adds a length: 180 days. A policy for a two-week holiday does not match that wording.

Is health insurance mandatory for UAE residents?

Yes in Abu Dhabi and Dubai, and the u.ae portal says who carries the duty. Abu Dhabi and Dubai split that duty differently.

Emirate Who must insure whom (u.ae, checked 30 October 2026)
Abu Dhabi Employers and sponsors insure their employees and their families: 1 spouse and 3 children under 18
Dubai Employers insure their employees; sponsors insure their resident dependants

So in Abu Dhabi, a family of one spouse and up to three children under 18 falls under the employer's or sponsor's duty. The portal does not say who covers anyone beyond that. In Dubai the family is the sponsor's responsibility, which for most expatriate families means the working parent who sponsors them.

The portal adds that the extent of cover for employees' dependants depends on the employee's salary, designation and similar factors.

When you sponsor a dependant's residence visa, the u.ae list of required documents includes "Medical insurance or health card", alongside a medical fitness certificate for dependants over 18.

The 2025 rule for private-sector workers and domestic workers

From 1 January 2025, employers must buy a health insurance policy before applying to issue or renew the residency of private-sector employees and domestic workers. The u.ae page, citing the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE), gives the details of the basic package:

Item Basic package (u.ae, updated 8 September 2026)
Where it applies Mandatory in Abu Dhabi and Dubai; the page says it will extend to Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah
Exception Work permits issued before 1 January 2024 that are still valid; insurance becomes mandatory at the next residency renewal
Premium AED 320 a year
Policy term Two years; the second year's premium can be refunded if the visa is cancelled
Ages 1 to 64; over 64, a medical disclosure form with recent medical reports
Inpatient co-payment 20%, at most AED 500 per visit and AED 1,000 a year including medicines
Outpatient co-payment 25%, at most AED 100 per visit; none for a follow-up within 7 days for the same condition
Medicines Co-payment up to 30%, annual cap AED 1,500
Network 7 hospitals, 46 clinics and medical centres, 45 pharmacies

The page also says workers' dependants can get the same benefits and pricing as set out in the policy, and that there is no waiting period for chronic illnesses.

Residence rules that sit next to the insurance rule

A few rules from the u.ae residence visa page affect people who hold insurance through their residency:

  • Applicants aged 18 and over need a medical fitness test for a residence visa.
  • If an expatriate resident lives outside the UAE for more than 180 days continuously, the residence visa is cancelled automatically. The page lists exceptions, such as students abroad and people sent abroad for treatment.
  • After a residence permit is cancelled or expires, residents get a grace period of up to six months, depending on their category.

UAE nationals: Thiqa and Enaya

For completeness: Abu Dhabi's Thiqa programme gives full cover to UAE nationals and to non-UAE nationals sponsored by a UAE national family member, under Department of Health criteria. In Dubai, the Enaya scheme covers Dubai citizens and Dubai Government employees.

Comparing other countries

Schengen visas use a fixed EU rule, €30,000 of cover valid in every Schengen state. Turkey asks for insurance in the residence permit file but exempts applicants under 18 and over 65: see Turkey residence permit health insurance. The five-year visa above asks for a passport check too, so our guide to passport validity requirements may help. If you plan to use a local eSIM there, first check whether your phone supports eSIM.

The issuing authority decides every visa and residency. This page reports what u.ae and GDRFA Dubai published as of 30 October 2026; confirm your own case with the authority handling your file, or on u.ae.

Questions people ask

Do I need health insurance for a UAE tourist visa?

For the visas we checked, yes. GDRFA Dubai lists medical insurance valid in the UAE for its 30- and 60-day single-entry tourist visa, and the u.ae portal lists a policy valid for 180 days for the five-year multiple-entry tourist visa. Other visas and emirates may word it differently; check the issuing authority's service page.

Does my travel insurance from home count?

The wording on both official pages is a policy valid or applicable in the UAE. A policy that excludes the UAE or the region does not meet that. Ask the airline, hotel or agent filing your visa whether they accept your policy.

Who pays for my family's insurance if I live in Dubai?

The u.ae portal says that in Dubai employers must insure their employees, and sponsors must get insurance for their resident dependants. If you sponsor your family, their cover is your duty, not your employer's.

How much is the basic policy for workers?

The u.ae portal, citing MoHRE, gives AED 320 a year for the basic package for private-sector employees and domestic workers, with a two-year policy term. That is the figure on 30 October 2026.
