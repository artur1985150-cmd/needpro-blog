In short

  • Valid health insurance is part of a Turkish residence permit file (ikamet).
  • The Presidency of Migration Management says applicants under 18 and over 65 are not obliged to hold insurance for short-term and student permits; they pick the exemption option in e-İkamet.
  • For a family permit, the sponsor's policy must cover every family member in the application.
  • Turkey's state scheme, GSS, opens only after one year of residence; foreign students can join within three months of first enrolment.

Yes, a Turkish residence permit (ikamet) needs valid health insurance, but not for everyone. On 30 October 2026 the Presidency of Migration Management says applicants under 18 and over 65 are not obliged to hold it for short-term and student permits. The state scheme, GSS, is not an option on arrival: it opens after one year of residence, with an exception for students.

Most of what ranks for this question comes from universities, written for exchange students, and from companies selling "residence permit insurance". Useful, both of them. Neither quotes the Migration Management wording, though, and that wording is where the exemptions live.

Who needs health insurance for a residence permit?

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs sets the starting point for anyone arriving on a visa: "All applicants are required to have a medical insurance that will be valid during their stay in Türkiye." The same page says that people who want to stay longer than 90 days should apply for a short-term residence permit.

For the permit itself, the Presidency of Migration Management (Göç İdaresi, often shortened to PMM or DGMM) treats valid health insurance as part of the file. Its frequently asked questions page, which we read in Turkish, adds who is exempt and how the form handles it.

Applicant What the PMM FAQ says (checked 30 October 2026)
Short-term or student permit, aged 18 to 65 Valid health insurance required
Short-term or student permit, under 18 or over 65 No obligation to hold insurance; choose the "under 18 and over 65" option in e-İkamet
Family residence permit The sponsor must hold valid insurance covering every family member
Long-term permit, over 65 A document showing you could not get insurance is enough
Health, food or housing costs paid by a Turkish public body Insurance and proof of means are not required

Insurance sellers often write "mandatory for ages 18 to 65" and stop there. The PMM wording goes the other way round: everyone needs it, and then it names who does not.

Under 18 or over 65: what to enter in e-İkamet

The e-İkamet online form still shows these applicants the insurance fields. The FAQ answers that exact question: in short-term and student applications, choose the option for applicants under 18 and over 65 in the insurance type field.

Two limits sit next to this rule in the same answer:

  • For a family residence permit the sponsor's policy must cover all family members, children included. The age exemption does not replace that.
  • A foreigner under 18 cannot get a long-term permit without income or assets independent of their parents.

What kind of policy, and for how long?

The PMM FAQ does not name approved insurers or minimum limits on the pages we read, so we do not either. The provincial migration directorate decides each file and can ask for more documents. Ask the directorate handling your application, or the insurer, whether a given policy is accepted before you pay.

The FAQ does answer one practical question about duration. Short-term permits are given for at most two years, and the length is at the administration's discretion. If you ask for two years and your policy lasts one, the FAQ says you can request two years when the insurer issues a 1+1 year policy, or when you take out two years of GSS.

When the insurance does not match in e-İkamet

A common error is the insurance mismatch message. The FAQ's answer:

  1. Contact your insurer and get the policy number, the insurer's name, the renewal number and the start and end dates.
  2. Enter exactly those details.
  3. If your policy shows no renewal number, enter 0 for a first policy with that insurer, 1 for the second, 2 for the third.
  4. Try entering the policy number with digits only, without the letters printed on it.
  5. If it still fails, the FAQ sends you back to the insurer.

Can a foreigner join Turkey's state insurance (GSS)?

Yes, but not on arrival. The PMM health brochure is direct: to take out general health insurance (genel sağlık sigortası, GSS), "Türkiye'de 1 yıl ikamet etme şartı aranır", meaning one year of residence in Turkey is required. Until then you rely on private cover.

The brochure lists three groups treated differently:

  • Foreign students can take out GSS without the one-year condition if they apply within three months of first enrolling at their school.
  • People with an international protection application or status, Syrians under temporary protection and people recognised as stateless are registered in GSS, and the state pays their premiums.
  • People insured in another country's state scheme are not covered in Turkey on that basis, except where one of Turkey's bilateral social security agreements applies. The brochure says these agreements exist with 28 countries.

To apply for GSS, the brochure says you go to the provincial social security directorate with:

  1. Your residence permit document.
  2. A letter, original or copy, from your home country's social security institution or labour attaché showing your social security status for GSS purposes.
  3. The undertaking (request) form.

Once you are in, the brochure says your spouse and children under 18 can use the cover too. Children in high school or an apprenticeship are covered until 20, unmarried children in higher education until 25, and parents you support can also be included. GSS ends when your residence permit ends, among other reasons the brochure lists.

A checklist before you file

  1. Check your age group against the table above.
  2. If you need a policy, make sure it runs for the whole permit period you ask for (or is a 1+1 year policy for two years).
  3. For a family permit, check that the sponsor's policy names every family member in the application.
  4. Keep the policy number, insurer name, renewal number and dates at hand for e-İkamet.
  5. If you plan to switch to GSS later, note the date your first year of residence ends.

Questions people ask

Is health insurance mandatory for a Turkish residence permit?

For most applicants, yes. The Presidency of Migration Management lists valid health insurance among the conditions, and the e-İkamet form asks for the policy details. Applicants under 18 and over 65 on short-term and student permits are the stated exception.

I am over 65. What do I put in the insurance field of e-İkamet?

Choose the option for applicants under 18 and over 65 in the insurance type field, instead of entering a policy. That is what the Migration Management FAQ says to do, as checked on 30 October 2026.

Can I join Turkey's public health insurance (GSS) straight away?

Usually not. The Migration Management health brochure says GSS requires one year of residence in Turkey. Foreign students can apply within three months of first enrolling at their school without waiting the year.

I want a two-year short-term permit but my policy lasts one year. What then?

The Migration Management FAQ says you can request two years if the insurer issues a 1+1 year policy, or if you take out two years of GSS.

e-İkamet says my insurance does not match. How do I fix it?

The FAQ says to get the policy number, insurer name, renewal number and start and end dates from your insurer and enter those. If the policy shows no renewal number, enter 0 for a first policy with that insurer, 1 for the second, 2 for the third.
